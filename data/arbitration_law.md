# Arbitration Law in India — Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996

## Overview
Based on UNCITRAL Model Law. Amended significantly in 2015, 2019, and 2021.
Governs: Domestic arbitration, international commercial arbitration, enforcement of foreign awards, conciliation.

## Arbitration Agreement (Section 7)
- Must be in writing (email/fax sufficient).
- Can be a clause in a main contract or a separate agreement.
- Must refer existing or future disputes to arbitration.
- Separability: Arbitration clause survives even if main contract is void (Doctrine of Separability).

## Appointment of Arbitrator
- Parties free to agree on procedure.
- Default: 3 arbitrators (each party appoints 1; two appointed arbitrators appoint the third).
- Supreme Court/High Court appoints if parties fail (Section 11).
- **2019 Amendment**: Arbitration Council of India (ACI) to maintain panel; grading of arbitral institutions.
- Challenges to arbitrator: Justifiable doubts about independence/impartiality (Section 12); mandatory disclosures required.

## Conduct of Arbitral Proceedings
- Equal treatment of parties (Section 18).
- Parties free to agree on procedure; tribunal has discretion otherwise.
- **Place of Arbitration**: Determines "seat" and law governing the arbitration.
- **Interim Measures (Section 17)**: Tribunal can grant interim relief; Section 9 allows court to grant before/during/after arbitration.
- Fast-track arbitration: Award in 6 months (Section 29B).

## Arbitral Award
- Must be in writing, signed by majority, reasoned (unless parties agree otherwise).
- **Domestic Award**: Treated as a decree of the court (Section 36).
- **Setting Aside (Section 34)**:
  - Incapacity of party.
  - Arbitration agreement invalid.
  - No proper notice / inability to present case.
  - Award beyond scope of submission.
  - Composition of tribunal not in accordance with agreement.
  - Subject matter not arbitrable under Indian law.
  - **Public Policy**: Most litigated ground — award contrary to fundamental policy of Indian law, interests of India, justice or morality, or induced by fraud/corruption.
  - Time limit: 3 months from receipt of award + 30 days' condonable delay.

## International Commercial Arbitration
- Applies when at least one party is a foreign national/body corporate.
- Seat of arbitration may be in India or abroad.
- **Foreign Awards**: Governed by New York Convention (Section 44) or Geneva Convention.
- Enforcement of foreign award refused only on grounds similar to Section 34 + non-arbitrability.

## Conciliation (Part III)
- Conciliator assists parties to reach settlement.
- Settlement agreement has the status of an arbitral award.

## Key Amendments

### 2015 Amendment
- Section 12(5): Persons with relationships to parties disqualified as arbitrators.
- Section 29A: Time limit of 12 months for award (extendable by 6 months by parties; court after that).
- Section 34(2A): Additional ground for setting aside — patent illegality (domestic arbitration only).

### 2019 Amendment
- Automatic stay of award on challenge if award is induced by fraud/corruption.
- Arbitration Council of India (ACI) established.
- Confidentiality provisions (Section 42A).

### 2021 Amendment
- Automatic stay on award pending challenge in some cases modified.
- Unconditional stay if prima facie case of fraud shown.

## Arbitrability in India
**Non-Arbitrable**: Criminal matters, matrimonial disputes, insolvency, testamentary matters, trust disputes, tenancy (under Rent Control Acts), fraud (partially).
**Arbitrable**: Commercial disputes, IP licensing disputes (Vidya Drolia v. Durga Trading Corp, 2021).

## Key Case Laws
- **BALCO v. Kaiser Aluminium (2012)**: Seat theory adopted; Indian courts' jurisdiction based on seat.
- **Bharat Aluminium Co. v. Kaiser Aluminium (2012)** (BALCO): Part I of Act does not apply to foreign-seated arbitrations.
- **Vidya Drolia v. Durga Trading Corp (2021)**: Landlord-tenant disputes under Transfer of Property Act are arbitrable; not under Rent Control Acts.
- **Perkins Eastman v. HSCC (2019)**: Person who is a party cannot appoint sole arbitrator.
- **Amazon v. Future Retail (2021)**: Emergency arbitrator awards are enforceable in India.
